          What your agents can reach

          Things on this Mac, not accounts somewhere else: nothing here signs in to anything and there is no token to expire. Everything is off until you switch it on, and these are run by Errand rather than by the agent, so walling an agent into its own folder does not decide them. Mail and Calendar stay on this machine. Chrome does not: reading a page sends a request out, from your browser, signed in as you, and Chrome has a second switch of its own that has to be on as well. Read that row before you turn it on.

            When Errand runs

            Everything Errand does on its own it does while it is open: a routine fires from a clock in this app, a watch looks while this window is there, and a command left running stops when this stops. Starting at login is not a machine of its own, and a Mac that is asleep or off is still asleep or off. It is the difference between standing jobs that survive a restart and standing jobs that do not.
